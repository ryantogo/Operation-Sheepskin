# Operation Shearling (2026)

Briefing text, scoring and designer notes for `build_shearling_2026.lua`, the modern-context companion to *Operation Sheepskin Gone Hot*. The two sections marked **Paste** go into the scenario editor; everything below them is reference for the designer.

---

## Paste: Scenario Description

**Anguilla, 19 March 2026. A hypothetical scenario.**

Fifty-seven years after Operation Sheepskin, Anguilla secedes again. A disputed referendum, a collapsed tourist season and a Committee of Public Safety in The Valley produce a unilateral declaration of independence, and this time the islanders have had months to prepare.

They have spent their money the way small states always do: second-hand, through intermediaries, from whoever would sell. Two Kfir C.12s refurbished in Israel after Colombian service; two Argentine Pampa trainers; a pair of Israeli-built Dabur patrol boats from the Argentine Navy; a retired Hellenic Navy Type 148 missile boat and an Exocet coastal battery from the same source; Chinese drones and FN-6 missiles; French Mistrals bought through a third country. A volunteer cadre, Cuban-trained and Venezuelan-funded, has come to show them how to use it.

London answers the way it did in 1969: frigates, paratroopers and the Metropolitan Police. The plan is the same. The reception is not.

*Single player: choose the United Kingdom. Real-time multiplayer (CMO v1.10): co-op with both players on the United Kingdom against the AI, or head-to-head with one player per side. Recommended: play the full twelve hours; most games are decided within six.*

## Paste: United Kingdom Briefing

**SITUATION.** At 0500 local the amphibious task group is off Road Bay. The RAF detachment at V.C. Bird, Antigua (95 nm south-east) has four Typhoons on alert. The Anguillan air arm is believed to be at Clayton J. Lloyd International (Wallblake) and its boats along the north and east coasts.

**ENEMY (assessed).**
- Air: two Kfir C.12 (Python and Derby capable), two IA-63 Pampa light attack aircraft, one Wing Loong II armed drone, one Hermes 450 surveillance drone, all at Wallblake.
- Sea: one Type 148 missile boat (Exocet) and two Dabur patrol boats, last seen near Island Harbour and Crocus Bay.
- Coastal defence: an MM.40 Exocet battery somewhere inland, probably cued by a surface-search radar on Crocus Hill.
- Air defence: Mistral and FN-6 MANPADS, ZU-23 and heavy machine-gun technicals.
- Ground: Anguillian Defence Force militia at Sandy Ground, Wallblake and The Quarter; a volunteer cadre platoon and an 81mm mortar platoon.

**FRIENDLY FORCES.**
- HMS Iron Duke (Type 23): 4.5-inch Mk 8 gunfire support, Sea Ceptor, NSM, Wildcat 1.
- HMS Dauntless (Type 45): area air defence, Wildcat 2.
- RFA Mounts Bay: LCVP Alpha, LCVP Bravo and LCU Foxtrot in the well; Merlin 1 and Merlin 2 on deck.
- HMS Medway (River Batch 2): close escort and inshore patrol.
- RAF: Typhoon 1 to 4 at V.C. Bird, Antigua. Typhoon 1 and 2 are assigned to the Anguilla CAP.
- A rifle company of 2 PARA with Company HQ (JTAC), a mortar section, and a Metropolitan Police Territorial Support Group.

**MISSION.** Land 2 PARA, secure Sandy Ground, Wallblake, The Quarter and The Valley, and put the Metropolitan Police into The Valley to restore civil authority. Keep the task group afloat.

**EXECUTION.**
1. *Launch.* Launch the landing craft from Mounts Bay. LCVP Alpha (1 Platoon) and LCVP Bravo (2 Platoon) land at BEACH Sandy Ground; LCU Foxtrot carries the company reserve and the mortar section. Merlin 1 and Merlin 2 lift 3 Platoon and its fire support group to LZ Wallblake.
2. *Follow-on waves.* Return LCVP Alpha to Mounts Bay to embark Company HQ (JTAC); return LCVP Bravo to embark the police, who will not board until Sandy Ground is secure.
3. *Advance.* Clear Wallblake, then The Quarter, then enter The Valley.
4. *End state.* The scenario ends ten minutes after the police reach The Valley with the town in British hands.

**HOW THE LIFTS WORK.** Troops go ashore automatically when a loaded landing craft comes within about 600 m of the BEACH marker, or when a loaded Merlin comes within about 800 m of the LZ marker below 350 m. An empty craft embarks its next wave when it comes back within about 700 m of Mounts Bay. A craft lost while loaded takes its troops with it.

**RULES OF ENGAGEMENT.** Do not fire first. The task group and the RAF detachment start on WEAPONS HOLD on every axis (self-defence only), so nobody shoots the Kfir patrol down on their own initiative. The side is released to WEAPONS TIGHT automatically when a British unit is hit or a hostile launch is detected, or after 30 minutes if nobody has fired. Destroying an Anguillan unit before then costs 20 points.

**POLITICAL GUIDANCE.** Killing the volunteer cadre is acceptable; killing Anguillian militia costs points, and so does any damage to the church, school, hospital, yacht or fishing boats. Wrecking the island's only international airport also costs points; capture it instead.

## Paste: Anguilla Briefing (head-to-head multiplayer only)

**SITUATION.** At 0500 local a British amphibious task group is off Road Bay: a Type 23, a Type 45, RFA Mounts Bay and a patrol ship. Typhoons are at Antigua, 95 nm south-east.

**YOUR FORCES.**
- Air (Wallblake): two Kfir C.12, two Pampas, one Wing Loong II, one Hermes 450.
- Sea: ANS *Sombrero* (Type 148, Exocet) at Island Harbour; ANS *Dog Island* and ANS *Scrub Island* (Daburs) in Crocus Bay.
- Coast: the Exocet battery at Stoney Ground and its radar on Crocus Hill.
- Air defence: Mistrals at Wallblake, FN-6s on South Hill, a ZU-23 technical.
- Ground: militia at Sandy Ground, Wallblake and The Quarter; the volunteer cadre and mortar platoon; heavy machine guns and technicals.

**PRE-BUILT MISSIONS.** *Kfir CAP* and *Hermes Watch* are active. *Wing Loong Hunt*, *Pampa Strike* and *Naval Sortie* are inactive; activate them when you choose.

**RULES.** The British are on Weapons Hold until you open fire, so your first shot is free. If they shoot first, you score.

**SCORING.** You score for every British loss (the same values the British lose), +15 for each British ship hit, +20 each time the British destroy one of your units before you have fired, and +5 for each objective you still hold at every full hour. You lose points for each objective the British secure and for each unit you lose. Civilian losses cost both sides 20 points, whoever caused them.

## How the 1969 plan maps onto 2026

| 1969 | 2026 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| HMS Minerva, gunfire | HMS Iron Duke | Type 23 with the 4.5-inch Mk 8 |
| HMS Rothesay, gunfire | HMS Dauntless | The modern threat is aircraft and missiles, so the second escort is an air-defence ship that still carries a 4.5-inch gun |
| Two LCVPs | Two LCVP Mk.V and an LCU Mk.10 from RFA Mounts Bay | Mounts Bay is the ship that actually serves on Caribbean station |
| Two Wasps | Two Merlin HC.4 lifting, two Wildcat HMA.2 armed | |
| (none) | HMS Medway | The River Batch 2 patrol ship based in the Caribbean |
| (none) | Four Typhoons at Antigua | The only way to contest the Kfirs; the Typhoons start on Hold and need an armed loadout |
| DShKs, mortar, technicals, Cuban cadre | HMG platoons, mortar platoon, three technicals, volunteer cadre | Same positions, same narrative beats |
| (none) | Kfirs, Pampas, drones, Type 148, Daburs, Exocet battery, MANPADS | The modern "volunteer" package that makes the naval and air fight |

Balance is kept in moderation: two fighters, two light attack aircraft, two drones, three boats and one coastal battery against two escorts, a patrol ship and four Typhoons. The British should win; the Exocet battery and the boats decide how much it costs.

## Real-time multiplayer notes

- **Co-op:** both players take the United Kingdom; Anguilla stays on the AI with its scripted timeline and the withdrawal at The Quarter. Routine news (troops ashore, waves embarked, notable kills) appears as short map notifications instead of pop-ups, so one player's pop-up does not interrupt the other. Critical news (ROE change, objectives, losses, the end) is still a pop-up.
- **Head-to-head:** one player per side. The scripted AI timeline and the withdrawal are switched off for a human Anguilla; the pre-built missions are left for that player. All news is side-addressed so neither player sees the other's messages.
- **Detection:** the scripts ask the game whether the session is multiplayer and whether Anguilla is human; the first British pop-up shows the result. On builds older than v1.10 they fall back to single-player behaviour.
- **Script load:** one timer event every 15 to 30 seconds, plus events that fire only when something is destroyed, a ship is hit, or the first hostile launch is seen. Ground-unit and aircraft hits, and launch detection, each fire once.
- See [`docs/rtmp-test-plan.md`](../../docs/rtmp-test-plan.md) for what to verify in a live session.

## Loadouts to set after building

The console lists every aircraft placed on a placeholder loadout. Suggested choices:

- **Typhoon 1 to 4:** two on air-to-air (Meteor and ASRAAM), two on strike (Paveway IV) for the radar, the Exocet battery and the technicals.
- **Wildcat 1, Wildcat 2:** Sea Venom (or Martlet) for the boats.
- **Merlin 1, Merlin 2:** troop transport.
- **Kfir 1, Kfir 2:** Python 5 and Derby.
- **Pampa 1, Pampa 2:** rockets or bombs.
- **Wing Loong 1:** its precision-guided missiles.
- **Hermes 1:** surveillance.

If you know the loadout IDs, enter them in the `LOADOUTS` table at the top of the builder and they are applied directly.

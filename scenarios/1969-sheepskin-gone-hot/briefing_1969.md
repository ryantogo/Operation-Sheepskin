# Operation Sheepskin Gone Hot (1969)

Briefing text, scoring and designer notes for `build_sheepskin_1969.lua`. The two sections marked **Paste** go into the scenario editor; everything below them is reference for the designer.

---

## Paste: Scenario Description

**Anguilla, 19 March 1969. A hypothetical scenario.**

On 19 March 1969, British paratroopers and police landed on Anguilla to end the island's unilateral secession. The real Operation Sheepskin met no resistance; Anguillians greeted the troops on the beach, and the "invasion" became a byword for imperial farce.

This scenario diverges at a single point. Although the historical Anguillian Defence Force buried its rifles before the landing, here it digs in instead, because a small detachment of Cuban "volunteers" arrived in the weeks after the Whitlock fiasco. Havana has supplied what the islanders lacked: DShK heavy machine guns, a mortar, improvised technicals, a cadre of men who have seen fire before, and (rumour insists) a torpedo boat and an old Trojan trainer flown by a Cuban pilot.

The British plan stays exactly as written. Only the reception changes.

*Single player: choose the United Kingdom. Real-time multiplayer (CMO v1.10): co-op with both players on the United Kingdom against the AI, or head-to-head with one player per side. Recommended: play the full twelve hours; most games are decided within six.*

## Paste: United Kingdom Briefing

**SITUATION.** The task force rides at anchor in Road Bay at 0500 local. Intelligence describes a lightly armed militia of a few dozen men. Nobody aboard expects a shot.

**ENEMY (assessed, unconfirmed).**
- Anguillian Defence Force militia, perhaps three platoons, around Sandy Ground, Wallblake and The Quarter.
- Reports of foreign volunteers with heavy machine guns. Reports of a small fast boat in Crocus Bay and an aircraft at Wallblake. Treat both as unconfirmed.

**FRIENDLY FORCES.**
- HMS Minerva and HMS Rothesay: 4.5-inch naval gunfire support, Seacat, one Wasp each.
- RFA Sir Lancelot with LCVP 1 and LCVP 2 alongside.
- A rifle company of 2 PARA: 1 and 2 Platoons in the LCVPs, 3 Platoon and its GPMG section in the Wasps, Company HQ (with a naval gunfire observer) and the company reserve aboard Sir Lancelot.
- A Metropolitan Police detachment aboard Sir Lancelot.
- One RAF Shackleton MR.3 from Coolidge Field, Antigua, flying the Maritime Watch patrol.

**MISSION.** Land 2 PARA, secure Sandy Ground, Wallblake, The Quarter and The Valley, and put the Metropolitan Police into The Valley to restore civil authority.

**EXECUTION.**
1. *Landing.* LCVP 1 (1 Platoon) and LCVP 2 (2 Platoon) land at BEACH Sandy Ground. Wasp 1 and Wasp 2 lift 3 Platoon and its GPMG section to LZ Wallblake.
2. *Follow-on waves.* Return the empty LCVPs alongside Sir Lancelot: LCVP 1 embarks Company HQ (NGFO) and the reserve; LCVP 2 embarks the police, who will not board until Sandy Ground is secure.
3. *Advance.* Clear Wallblake, then The Quarter, then enter The Valley.
4. *End state.* The scenario ends ten minutes after the police reach The Valley with the town in British hands.

**HOW THE LIFTS WORK.** Troops go ashore automatically when a loaded LCVP comes within about 600 m of the BEACH marker, or when a loaded Wasp comes within about 800 m of the LZ marker below 350 m. Order the Wasps to low altitude for the run in. An empty craft embarks its next wave when it comes back within about 700 m of its parent ship. A craft lost while loaded takes its troops with it.

**RULES OF ENGAGEMENT.** Do not fire first. The task force starts on WEAPONS HOLD on every axis (self-defence only). It is released to WEAPONS TIGHT automatically when a British unit is hit or a hostile launch is detected, or after 30 minutes if nobody has fired. Destroying an Anguillan unit before then costs 20 points.

**POLITICAL GUIDANCE.** Whitehall wants a police action, not a war. Killing Cubans is acceptable; killing Anguillian militia costs points, and so does any damage to the church, school, hospital or fishing boats. If the defenders at The Quarter can be persuaded to leave rather than die, so much the better.

**SCORING.**

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Sandy Ground / Wallblake / The Quarter / The Valley secured | +15 / +20 / +15 / +30 |
| Police ashore / police in The Valley | +10 / +10 |
| Defenders withdraw from The Quarter | +15 |
| DShK or mortar destroyed | +10 each |
| Technical destroyed / Cuban platoon destroyed | +5 each |
| Torpedo boat / T-28 destroyed | +15 / +10 |
| Anguillian militia platoon destroyed | -5 each |
| Wallblake airstrip destroyed | -10 |
| Civilian building or boat destroyed | -20 each |
| Paratroop platoon / Company HQ / police lost | -25 / -20 / -30 |
| GPMG section / Wasp / LCVP / Shackleton lost | -10 / -15 / -10 / -30 |
| Any ship hit (first hit per ship) / ship sunk | -15 / -100 |
| Enemy destroyed before the ROE release | -20 each |

## Paste: Anguilla Briefing (head-to-head multiplayer only)

**SITUATION.** At 0500 local two British frigates and a landing ship are at anchor in Road Bay. Landing craft are forming up off Sandy Ground and helicopters are warming up on the frigates' decks. London expects a walkover.

**YOUR FORCES.**
- Sandy Ground: a DShK on the ridge, a militia platoon in the village, the 82mm mortar on the reverse slope.
- Wallblake: a DShK covering the strip, a roadblock technical, a militia platoon, and the Cuban T-28 Trojan.
- The Quarter: the Cuban volunteer platoon, a militia platoon and the Valley road technical.
- Crocus Bay: the torpedo boat *Libertad*.

**PRE-BUILT MISSIONS.** *T-28 Harassment* and *Torpedo Run* (anti-shipping patrols over Road Bay) are inactive; activate them when you choose.

**RULES.** The British are on Weapons Hold until you open fire. Make them land first if you can; if they shoot first, you score.

**SCORING.** You score for every British loss (the same values the British lose), +15 for each British ship hit, +20 each time the British destroy one of your units before you have fired, and +5 for each objective you still hold at every full hour. You lose points for each objective the British secure and for each unit you lose. Civilian losses cost both sides 20 points, whoever caused them.

## Changes from the narrative (kept deliberately small)

The narrative is a walkover with casualties. To give the naval and air game something to do, without turning a police action into a Cold War battle, the builder adds:

- **Anguilla:** one P-6 torpedo boat with a Cuban crew hiding in Crocus Bay; one ex-FAR T-28A Trojan at Wallblake; a second militia platoon at Wallblake.
- **United Kingdom:** RFA Sir Lancelot as the source of the landing craft and the follow-on waves; one Shackleton MR.3 for maritime surveillance; a Company HQ party that doubles as the naval gunfire observer.
- **Mechanics:** a do-not-fire-first ROE, political penalties for Anguillian and civilian deaths, and a scripted withdrawal at The Quarter that rewards the narrative outcome over annihilation.

Everything else (two frigates, two Wasps, a rifle company in three platoons plus reserve, the police, two DShKs, the mortar, the two technicals, the Cuban cadre at The Quarter) follows the narrative.

## Real-time multiplayer notes

- **Co-op:** both players take the United Kingdom; Anguilla stays on the AI with its scripted timeline and the withdrawal at The Quarter. Routine news (troops ashore, waves embarked, notable kills) appears as short map notifications instead of pop-ups, so one player's pop-up does not interrupt the other. Critical news (ROE change, objectives, losses, the end) is still a pop-up.
- **Head-to-head:** one player per side. The scripted AI timeline and the withdrawal are switched off for a human Anguilla; the pre-built missions are left for that player. All news is side-addressed so neither player sees the other's messages.
- **Detection:** the scripts ask the game whether the session is multiplayer and whether Anguilla is human; the first British pop-up shows the result. On builds older than v1.10 they fall back to single-player behaviour.
- **Script load:** one timer event every 15 to 30 seconds, plus events that fire only when something is destroyed, a ship is hit, or the first hostile launch is seen. Ground-unit and aircraft hits, and launch detection, each fire once.
- See [`docs/rtmp-test-plan.md`](../../docs/rtmp-test-plan.md) for what to verify in a live session.

## Loadouts to set after building

The console lists every aircraft placed on a placeholder loadout. Suggested choices:

- **Wasp 1, Wasp 2:** AS.12 missiles (they can then hunt the torpedo boat or the technicals once the troops are down).
- **Shackleton 1:** a maritime patrol or anti-shipping loadout.
- **Volunteer T-28:** gun pods and rockets.

If you know the loadout IDs, enter them in the `LOADOUTS` table at the top of the builder and they are applied directly.
